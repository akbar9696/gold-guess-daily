# Terms of Use — Gold Guess

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Gold Guess ("the App") is a daily guessing game built for Reddit with Devvit Web. By playing the game, you agree to these terms.

## 1. The game

- Each day you guess the closing price of gold (XAU/USD, per troy ounce). You get **one guess per player per day**.
- Once locked in, a guess cannot be changed. The real close is revealed at midnight UTC, and the closest guess wins the day.
- Streaks, points, and leaderboard positions are tracked for gameplay only. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, prizes, or Reddit awards.

## 2. Price data

- The live gold price is fetched from a public market-data API (gold-api.com). If that feed is unreachable, the App shows a deterministic estimate that is clearly labeled **"fallback estimate"** and never presented as a real price.
- Market data may be delayed or inaccurate. The App is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.

## 3. Fair play

- One account, one guess per day. Do not use multiple accounts, bots, or automation to submit guesses.
- Content you share from the App (for example "Share my guess") must follow Reddit's Content Policy and the rules of the subreddit where it is posted.
- We may remove guesses or leaderboard entries that break these terms.

## 4. Changes and availability

- The App may change or stop working at any time, including after the hackathon it was built for. Daily reveals depend on Reddit's platform and the price feed being available.
- These terms may be updated; the version in the App's repository is the current one.
