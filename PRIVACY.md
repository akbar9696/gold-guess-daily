# Privacy Policy — Gold Guess

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Gold Guess ("the App") is a daily guessing game built for Reddit with Devvit Web. This policy explains what the App collects and why.

## 1. What we collect

When you play, the App stores, in Reddit's own storage (Devvit Redis) for this App only:

- Your Reddit **username and user ID** (provided by Reddit when you open the App).
- Your **daily guesses** (one per day), and derived game data: streak, best difference, total guesses, days played, total points, and wins.
- Leaderboard entries built from the above.

That is all. The App does **not** collect your email address, password, location, device information, browsing history, or payment details, and it does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting.

## 2. How data is used

Your data is used only to run the game: to enforce one guess per day, to reveal results, to keep your streak and stats, and to show the daily and all-time leaderboards. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## 3. Price feed

To show the live gold price, the App's server makes a request to a public market-data API (gold-api.com). That request contains **no user data** — only the request for the current XAU/USD price.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Game data is kept while the App is installed so your streak and history keep working. If the App is uninstalled from a subreddit, its stored data for that installation is removed by the platform. If you want your personal game data deleted, message the developer on Reddit at **u/CartoonistHot3081** and it will be removed as far as the platform allows.

## 5. Children

The App is intended for Reddit users who are at least 13 years old (or the age required by Reddit in your region).

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the App's repository. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the new policy.

## 7. Contact

Privacy questions: message the developer on Reddit at **u/CartoonistHot3081**.
